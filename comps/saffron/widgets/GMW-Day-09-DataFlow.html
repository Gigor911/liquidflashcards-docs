Rules that keep it safe
The widget never touches SwiftData, so iCloud never sees two writers. Ratings apply in ratedAt order with the review time they happened at.
Skip a queued rating if the card was reviewed in the app after ratedAt, or it was deleted. A rating for a card that’s no longer due still counts (early review).
A batch older than 24 hours shows the refresh state instead of cards. The log is cleared only after the app saves.
